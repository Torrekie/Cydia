# Native Home assets

These images are bundled snapshots of the historical Cydia Home artwork. They
are loaded locally by the UIKit Home controller; the app does not fetch the
legacy Home document or its artwork at runtime.

Source roots, captured 2026-08-27:

- `https://cydia.saurik.com/icon@2x/cydia7.png`
- `https://cydia.saurik.com/icon/facebook.png`
- `https://cydia.saurik.com/icon/twitter.png`
- `https://cydia.saurik.com/ui/ios~iphone/1.1/home/featured.png`
- `https://cydia.saurik.com/ui/ios~iphone/1.1/home/themes.png`
- `https://cydia.saurik.com/ui/ios~iphone/1.1/home/account.png`

The exact SHA-256 values are frozen by `tests/HomeControllerContractTests.sh`.
